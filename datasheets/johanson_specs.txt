Johanson Technology, Inc. (JTI) miniature RF ceramic chip antennas 
are made using Low Temperature Co-fired Ceramic (LTCC) technology 
which has the ability to embed low and high dielectric constants inside 
our antenna. This  enables our components to have high detuning 
resilience and stability over extreme temperatures (~2ppm).
